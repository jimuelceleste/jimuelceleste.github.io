I am a Research Assistant at the Collaboration for Open Science and Synthesis in Ecology and Evolution ([COSSEE](https://cossee.org)). Prior to joining COSSEE, I received my MSc in Computing Science from the University of Alberta. I worked with Prof. Eleni Stroulia on improving the reproducibility and replicability of machine learning speech experiments for cognitive health inference. At the same time, I contributed to the Artificial Intelligence for Pandemic and Epidemic Preparedness ([AI4PEP](https://ai4pep.org/philippines/)) Philippines where we co-developed an Influenza-Like Illness surveillance system using telehealth data and machine learning. This work is an extension of my experience as a Data Scientist for the community-based telehealth [Atipan Project](https://www.usacfi.net/atipan-project.html) in rural low-income and indigenous communities in Western Visayas, Philippines. I received my BSc in Computer Science from the University of the Philippines Diliman.
